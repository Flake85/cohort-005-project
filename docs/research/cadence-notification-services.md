# Hosted services for Cadence Notifications

Research for [Compare hosted services for Cadence Notifications](https://github.com/Flake85/cohort-005-project/issues/3), part of [Plan in-app Notifications for Cadence](https://github.com/Flake85/cohort-005-project/issues/1). Official sources checked on **2026-11-10**. This is a disposable research asset, not an approved vendor decision or implementation spec. No accounts, credentials, SDK installation, or paid purchases were used.

## Recommendation for the human decision

**Shortlist Courier, Novu Cloud, and Knock; evaluate Courier first if avoiding a large minimum paid commitment is the priority.** Courier documents a hosted feed, user-scoped expiring tokens, custom React UI, and usage pricing without a published base subscription. Novu offers a low paid entry price with finite feed history. Knock is particularly attractive if long-lived Notification history and detailed preferences matter enough to justify its paid minimum. The evidence for these judgments appears below; they are research recommendations, not a selection.

MagicBell and SuprSend are feasible alternatives. MagicBell has a smaller development allowance and a larger initial paid commitment. SuprSend's free feed includes branding, and preferences are now a Business feature. Neither should be rejected because of browser push support or preferences: browser push is out of scope, and preferences are not an agreed requirement. Four roles alone cannot establish whether any free quota is sufficient: event frequency and recipient fan-out remain undecided.

## Scope and application fit

Cadence needs a persistent **Notification Feed**, initially in-app only, for Students, Instructors, Team Admins, and Platform Admins. Future email is a comparison consideration. The local `package.json` uses React 19.2 and React Router 8.3; `app/routes/layout.app.tsx` resolves the current session person and renders a sidebar, scrolling main area, DevUI switcher, and transient Sonner toasts. `GLOSSARY.md` distinguishes a Team Admin from a Platform Admin. These are local observations, not proposed event rules.

All five products expose individual recipient identities. **Inference:** all four roles can use the same integration; Cadence must compute recipients and authorization from its own records. Vendor dashboard roles do not implement Cadence's roles. Do not create one shared feed for all Platform Admins: send one Notification per authorized person if that is the eventual event rule. Keep recipient identity stable across role changes; clear feed state and sockets when DevUI switches people or the session ends. Destination routes must still check current access.

## Free allowance, billing unit, and upgrade costs

Prices below are advertised USD amounts, before tax, without assuming volume or including future email provider charges. Let `M` be billable recipient/channel deliveries, and `R` subscriber workflow executions per billing month. Broadcasts to many people multiply usage; one business event is not necessarily one billed unit.

| [Knock pricing](https://knock.app/pricing)             | Developer: 10,000 messages/month                           | Starter: $250/month, 50,000 included; $0.005/additional message                                       | One successful delivery to one person on one channel. Suppressed/failed sends excluded. Starter cost: `$250 + .005 × max(0, M − 50,000)`. Free is not a hard cap: Knock contacts accounts that exceed it. Branding removal requires Starter.                                                                                             |
| [Novu pricing](https://www.novu.co/pricing/)           | 10,000 workflow runs/month; 20 workflows, two environments | Pro from $30/month for 30,000 runs; $1.20/1,000 additional                                            | Base Pro arithmetic: `$30 + .0012 × max(0, R − 30,000)`, subject to purchased tier. Team starts at $250 with 250,000 runs. Branding removal starts on Pro.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| [MagicBell pricing](https://www.magicbell.com/pricing) | Builder: 1,000 deliveries/month, one project               | Startup: $249/month, 50,000 included; $0.0025/additional delivery; five projects                      | Each person/channel delivery counts. Startup: `$249 + .0025 × max(0, M − 50,000)`. Extra Startup projects are $99 each. | [SuprSend pricing](https://www.suprsend.com/pricing)   | 10,000 notifications/month; branded feed                   | Essentials: $100/month **billed annually at $1,200**, 50,000 included; $0.002/additional notification | Each recipient/channel counts; downstream failures can still be billed. Essentials effective monthly arithmetic: `$100 + .002 × max(0, M − 50,000)`, plus annual upfront commitment. Business: $250/month billed annually, 50,000 included, $0.005/additional; preferences included there. Monthly-contract prices require confirmation. |

[Novu billing](https://docs.novu.co/platform/account/billing) defines one run as one workflow execution for one subscriber, regardless of channel count; development and production usage share the organization's allowance. Free rejects new runs with HTTP 402 after its limit. Paid Pro/Team continue with overages by default, but can pause at a configured usage limit. This makes its cost boundary more controllable, at the expense of delivery stopping when a limit is enforced.

SuprSend's pricing FAQ says delivery continues beyond Free and the company contacts the account; that is not an unlimited free entitlement. Knock likewise does not promise an automatic budget stop. Predictable unit rates and hard spending caps are separate questions. Estimate recipient fan-out after the event catalog is agreed, then compare formulas and the desired history period; no usage forecast has been invented here.

**Stored Notification ownership:** operationally, all five host the delivered feed content and engagement state described above. Cadence owns its event meaning, recipient selection, current domain data, and UI behavior. A hosted feed is a derived record of what was sent, not the authoritative record of a Purchase, Enrollment, or Refund Request. Recommendation: keep domain identifiers and links in payloads and retain any required event/retry record in Cadence. Decide explicitly whether vendor history is sufficient or a local Notification archive is needed. Export/import of all historical read states, deletion propagation, and contractual data rights were not verified; API access alone is not a migration guarantee. No legal ownership conclusion is implied.

## Per-person authentication and SSR

| Service    | Documented authentication                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | React/SSR assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Knock      | [Enhanced security](https://docs.knock.app/in-app-ui/security-and-authentication) is **off by default**. Enable it to require backend-signed RS256 user JWTs; expiry and access grants can restrict the person/endpoints/tenants. Public-key-only requests can otherwise impersonate people.                                                         | React components/headless hooks documented. A guaranteed pre-render/hydration path for Cadence's exact React Router version was not verified. Use a browser feed boundary and server credential endpoint; test imports and hydration. |
| Novu Cloud | [Production guide](https://docs.novu.co/platform/inbox/prepare-for-production) requires enabling the HMAC setting to protect feeds. Backend derives `subscriberHash = HMAC-SHA256(secret, subscriberId)`. **Inference:** that proof itself has no expiry claim and remains stable until identity/key changes; do not equate it to a short-lived JWT. | React provider/hooks documented. Exact React 19.2/Router SSR compatibility remains untested. Browser-only initialization is a plausible integration path, not a verified plug-in recipe.                                              |
| MagicBell  | [User JWT](https://www.magicbell.com/docs/api/authentication/user) uses HS256 and a project API key plus `user_external_id` or email; backend keeps the secret and sets expiry. [Project JWT](https://www.magicbell.com/docs/api/authentication/project) is for server administration/triggering, never browsers.                                    | React documented, but no explicit SSR guarantee found. Verify server imports, hydration, and browser state cleanup; REST gives an alternative custom data boundary.                                                                   |
| Courier    | [Auth guide](https://www.courier.com/docs/in-app/authenticate-users) mints expiring JWTs from the backend, bound by `user_id` and read/write scopes. Token issuance needs a Courier API call.                                                                                                                                                        | React docs explicitly discuss SSR frameworks but say feed components render client-side. The React 18+ package covers the project's major React version; Cadence-specific hydration still needs testing.                              |
| SuprSend   | [Client auth](https://docs.suprsend.com/docs/client-authentication) uses an optional enhanced security switch; enable it to mandate ES256 JWTs with `entity_id`, expiry, and optional tenant scope.                                                                                                                                                  | Current React provider/headless APIs documented. An explicit SSR compatibility guarantee was not found; test with the selected SDK versions.                                                                                          |

**Recommended integration boundary, not implementation:** derive the person exclusively from the Cadence server session, issue only that person's proof, keep privileged trigger keys on the server, and mount a client feed after authentication. A role or a browser-supplied person ID is not sufficient authentication. Client-only rendering can fit an SSR application without promising server-rendered historical Notifications. With no SDK spike/account test, none of these rows certifies full Cadence compatibility.

## Triggering, retries, and deduplication

| Service    | Backend trigger and idempotency                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Documented retries and limitations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Knock      | [Workflow trigger](https://docs.knock.app/send-notifications/triggering-workflows/api), REST/Node SDK, explicit recipients and payload. [`Idempotency-Key`](https://docs.knock.app/api-reference/overview) deduplicates workflow triggers for 24 hours; only successful responses are cached, and different parameters with a reused key error.                                                                                   | [Sending pipeline](https://docs.knock.app/send-notifications/delivering-notifications) retries transient/network/provider errors up to eight times over 30 minutes with backoff/jitter; dashboard can retry terminal failures. This does not retry a Cadence request that never reached Knock.                              |
| Novu Cloud | [Event trigger](https://docs.novu.co/api-reference/events/trigger-event), REST/Node SDK, subscriber recipients and payload. [Idempotency header](https://docs.novu.co/api-reference/idempotency) supports API-key POST/PATCH requests; success **and error** responses cached 24 hours, in-progress five minutes. Trigger `transactionId` also exists with tier-dependent retention; prefer an explicitly tested header contract. | [Retry policy](https://docs.novu.co/platform/developer/delivery-retries) distinguishes layers: in-app stores directly; no external provider call. Email/SMS/push/chat provider sends are attempted once, with failures recorded; Framework bridge calls retry three times. Do not assume general automatic channel retries. |
| MagicBell  | [REST v2](https://www.magicbell.com/docs/api/reference): create a broadcast with explicit recipients; broadcast is the API's term even for a targeted event. [API overview](https://www.magicbell.com/docs/api) documents idempotency for supported endpoints, caching the first result for 24 hours. Verify the exact chosen create endpoint during an account test.                                                             | Generic endpoint error guidance is documented. A precise automatic in-app retry/terminal recovery policy was not found; keep this unknown rather than importing another vendor's guarantee.                                                                                                                                 |
| Courier    | [`POST /send`](https://www.courier.com/docs/api-reference/send/send-a-message), Node SDK, explicit person and template/payload. Courier's [integration guide](https://www.courier.com/blog/design-studio-journeys-engineering-integration-guide) documents send idempotency keys. Exact server deduplication TTL was not established.                                                                                             | That guide describes SDK retries for connection failures, 408/409/429/5xx up to twice with backoff. [Platform documentation](https://www.courier.com/platform/notification-infrastructure) advertises downstream retries and failover; exact in-app retry schedule/guarantee needs confirmation.                            |
| SuprSend   | [`POST /trigger/`](https://docs.suprsend.com/reference/trigger-workflow-api), recipients/payload, up to 100 recipients per workflow request. [Idempotency docs](https://docs.suprsend.com/reference/idempotent-requests) give 24 hours. Reference names the field `$idempotency_key`, while the conceptual page says `idempotency_key`; use the selected SDK/API schema and confirm with a duplicate-trigger test.                | [Official transactional overview](https://www.suprsend.com/use-cases/transactional) advertises retries/failover but does not establish an exact in-app policy. A 202 Accepted response is not proof that a Notification reached the feed.                                                                                   |

**Inference for the eventual spec:** backend business writes and remote sends are separate operations. Vendor retries cannot repair a committed Cadence event that was never submitted. A stable event/recipient key plus a durable dispatch/outbox strategy should be considered if missing important outcomes is unacceptable; this research does not select or build that mechanism. Vendor deduplication windows also do not provide indefinite exactly-once delivery.

## Optional preferences and future email

- Knock stores category, channel/channel-type, and workflow preferences in a [PreferenceSet](https://docs.knock.app/preferences/overview), evaluated during workflows.
- Novu supports global channel and workflow/channel preferences, including critical workflows, through its [preferences model](https://docs.novu.co/platform/concepts/preferences). Do not assume categories must be an initial requirement or that critical means a Cadence authorization rule.
- MagicBell's [in-app guide](https://www.magicbell.com/docs/channels/in_app) describes category/channel preferences; current React includes a preference component.
- Courier's [JS preferences APIs](https://www.courier.com/docs/sdk-libraries/courier-js-web) expose topic opt-in/out/required and channel routing. Map templates to topics if that model is eventually wanted; exact free-plan entitlements should be confirmed.
- SuprSend's current pricing places channel and category preferences on Business, correcting the inherited claim that channel preferences start on Essentials. Free development remains viable for feed-only requirements if its branding is acceptable.

All five offer email alongside in-app: see [Knock channels/pricing](https://knock.app/pricing), [Novu billing](https://docs.novu.co/platform/account/billing), [MagicBell channels](https://www.magicbell.com/docs/channels/in_app), [Courier Send API](https://www.courier.com/docs/api-reference/send/send-a-message), and [SuprSend pricing FAQ](https://www.suprsend.com/pricing). Delivery-provider accounts/costs and sender configuration are additional work when email becomes in scope. Novu meters the subscriber run regardless of channel count; Knock, MagicBell, and SuprSend meter additional channel deliveries. Courier's cross-channel billing semantics need explicit confirmation. No email workflow is designed here.

## Revalidation of inherited course research

`docs/research/notification-vendors.md` is a historical starting point, not evidence of this fork's approval.

- Its Knock free allowance/Starter price and MagicBell allowance/Startup price still match the checked pricing pages. Its claim that Knock alone meets every criterion does not follow from this map's narrower in-app requirements.
- “Knock requires signed tokens in production” overstates enforcement: the setting must be enabled. Treat enabling per-person auth as essential configuration for every candidate that defaults to an insecure development mode.
- Novu's 24-hour activity-log retention must not be reported as 24-hour Notification Feed history. Its current pricing separately specifies 30-day Free feed history.
- SuprSend's current channel preference gate differs from the inherited report, and its old React inbox SDK has ended support. Current SDK documentation and names matter.
- Native browser push and category preferences no longer justify eliminating services because neither is an initial requirement. The inherited web-push claims were not revalidated because that channel is outside this investigation.

## Open facts for the next decision

The research question is answered sufficiently to compare services; these are explicit limits, not a hidden implementation approval:

1. **Human constraints:** acceptable feed history, vendor branding, base/annual spend, and acceptable behavior at a quota limit. Event catalog/fan-out and desired unread versus unseen badge semantics are still undecided.
2. **Courier:** confirm Business's continued free allowance, sends/tests/failure billing semantics, headless/free branding terms, unlimited unexpired feed history on the chosen plan, deduplication TTL, and in-app retry/recovery behavior. Its pricing page contains inconsistent old/new log-retention blocks; obtain a plan-specific answer if logs matter.
3. **Novu:** validate 30-/90-day history against the account's chosen plan and confirm how cached trigger errors should be recovered safely. HMAC replay/revocation properties may affect the auth decision if short-lived credentials are required.
4. **Knock:** accept the paid minimum if it is shortlisted; verify branding obligations for custom UI and enabled enhanced-security grants in the actual free account.
5. **MagicBell/SuprSend:** establish feed retention and detailed retry contracts. MagicBell's free overage treatment and SuprSend's total unread-count contract need confirmation. Avoid confusing SuprSend's opening/reset badge with an unread badge.
6. **All candidates:** a focused authorized account/SDK trial should test React Router SSR import/hydration, per-person isolation across all roles and DevUI switches, stale/expired credentials, duplicate sends, reconnect/count reconciliation, pagination, read/unread persistence, and chosen custom styling. Confirm historical export/deletion and data rights before deciding who owns the long-term archive.

No additional research follow-up was opened: these questions can first be prioritized in the existing human service/responsibility decision. This note changes no `app/` code; application typecheck/tests are not applicable to this documentation-only branch.
